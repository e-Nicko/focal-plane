# The shot

A shot lasts from 1.8 to 2.6 seconds and says one thing.

## One idea

Before drawing anything, write the shot as one sentence:
*the cursor turns the forecast on, and the forecast grows out of today.*
If the sentence needs "and then", it is two shots.

## Cause and effect in the same frame

The control and the thing it changes share the frame.
The viewer sees the click and its consequence with no cut between them
and perceives the link directly
([why it works](why-it-works.md)).
Focus starts on the cause and moves to the effect
([the lens](the-lens.md)).

## Scale contrast

Put something enormous next to something small.
In the horizon shot the slider is a thin line,
the projected revenue it changes is set at 210 px, nearer to the lens,
and the horizon itself, in 330 px digits, sits nearest of all.
A giant soft shape in the foreground reads as mass
and gives the frame depth for free.

## Diagonal, then frontal

Steep diagonals give energy:
the camera sits low over the page and off to one side (`elevation` 19–25°, `azimuth` ±45–60°).
Near-frontal shots give rest and legibility.
Alternate them, so the eye gets a pause after every run.

| Shot | Azimuth | Elevation | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| tabs | −58° | 20° | steep diagonal, speed |
| forecast | −17° | 41° | calm, near frontal |
| horizon | −46° | 25° | steep diagonal again |
| months | +36° | 28° | the other side, a new subject |
| anomaly | −7° | 55° | frontal, the answer |

## Negative space

Most of the frame is empty dark page.
The eye goes to the one lit thing.
Do not fill the frame with widgets:
a shot about a toggle shows the toggle, its label and the chart it changes.

## The example, shot by shot

| # | Shot | Length | The idea | Cause → effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | overview | 2.1 s | the page, and its headline number | focus finds $4.28M out of the blur |
| 2 | tabs | 1.8 s | pick Forecast | the hand glides, the underline follows, a click |
| 3 | forecast | 2.5 s | switch the forecast on | toggle → the projection grows out of today |
| 4 | horizon | 2.3 s | look further ahead | slider 30 → 90 days → the value rolls to $5.24M |
| 5 | months | 2.4 s | read the months | a click on a month → a giant readout rolls to it |
| 6 | anomaly | 2.6 s | find what does not fit | Line → Points → a ±2.5σ band, two days outside |
| 7 | outro | 2.4 s | the page after the story | forecast on, 90 days, outliers ringed |
